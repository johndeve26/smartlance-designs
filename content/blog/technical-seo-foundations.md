Technical SEO is the infrastructure behind discoverability. It will not replace useful content or a clear offer. But weak foundations can stop strong pages from performing.

## Make sure pages can be crawled and indexed

If important pages are blocked, orphaned, redirected incorrectly or buried in poor architecture, search engines may never evaluate them properly. Clean sitemaps, sensible robots rules and healthy internal links matter.

## Protect index quality

Duplicate content, conflicting canonicals and soft 404s create confusion. Every important URL should have a clear role and a preferred version.

## Treat performance as part of SEO

Slow pages frustrate users and can limit how well a site competes. Image optimization, lean front-end delivery and Core Web Vitals belong in the same conversation as rankings.

## Use structure and schema thoughtfully

Clear headings, clean URLs, internal links and relevant structured data help search engines understand page meaning. They also help people scan your content.

## Fix issues in priority order

Not every technical finding deserves equal urgency. Start with crawl blocks, indexation problems, broken critical paths and major speed issues. Then refine.
