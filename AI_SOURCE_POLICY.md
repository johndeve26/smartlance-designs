# AI Source Policy

## Hierarchy (approx.)

1. Official / primary documentation  
2. Government / standards  
3. Original research  
4. Reputable industry publications  
5. Expert secondary sources  
6. Community (experience/opinion)  

Do not blindly trust SERP order.

## Freshness

Time-sensitive topics (platform pricing/features, SEO guidance, stats, legal/regulatory) should use fresh research (`lastResearchAt`). Staleness is a review hint — not automatic “wrong.”

## Competitors

Use for coverage/gap awareness only. No mechanical structure cloning or paragraph rewriting.

## Copyright

Store URLs, titles, publishers, short snippets (where terms allow), and AI notes. Do not store full copyrighted articles for drafting.

## Claim ledger

Classify: `SUPPORTED_EXTERNAL` · `SUPPORTED_INTERNAL` · `GENERAL_KNOWLEDGE` · `EDITORIAL_OPINION` · `UNSUPPORTED` · `TIME_SENSITIVE`.

Numerical claims need sources or verified internal basis. Invented URLs are rejected.

## Citations

`RESEARCH_ONLY` (default) vs `VISIBLE_CITATIONS` (natural links / references matching Blog design — no fake academic footnotes unless the design supports them).
