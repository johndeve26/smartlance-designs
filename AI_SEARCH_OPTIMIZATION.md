# AI Search Optimization (Smartlance)

AI Search optimization in Editorial Studio is **editorial quality for extractability** — not a guaranteed GEO/AEO score and not a ranking promise.

## What Google states (operational summary)

- No special technical requirements beyond normal Search eligibility and SEO fundamentals for AI Overviews / AI Mode.  
- Do **not** invent: AI Overview schema, mandatory `llms.txt`, GEO tags, or “AI keyword density.”  
- Generative AI content is allowed when used appropriately; scaled low-value content primarily to manipulate Search may violate spam policies.

## What we optimize for

- Clear answers to core questions (answer-first when natural)  
- Unambiguous entity names and definitions  
- Supported factual claims  
- Original Smartlance value (flag commodity risk when none)  
- Clear section structure and concise extractable passages where natural  
- Appropriate attribution and internal links  
- Valid Article/BlogPosting structured data matching **visible** content (existing Insight schema)  

## What we do not do

- Fake SEO scores (0–100)  
- Automatic FAQ schema spam  
- Mass idea → publish pipelines  
- Fabricated search volume / GSC metrics when not integrated  

Use review severities: **PASS · WARNING · REVIEW · BLOCKER**.
