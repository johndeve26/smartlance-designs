# Client Success Portal UX

## Navigation

**Desktop**: Home · Projects · **Websites** · Approvals · Files · Documents · Billing

**Mobile bottom nav** (unchanged): Home · Projects · Files · Billing · More

**More menu**: Websites · Support · Approvals · Documents · Account

## My Websites

Card shows: name, domain, care status, last maintenance, open support count.

Actions: Open website · Request support · Visit website (external, validated URL).

## Website hub tabs

Overview · Care · Support

Overview order: status → care → open support → recent care → information.

## Support

Global list sections: Needs your response · Open · Resolved.

Detail: header, description, conversation, resolution confirmation.

## Home adaptation

- Active projects remain primary for delivery clients
- "Your Websites" (3–4 items) when managed websites exist
- Contextual subtitle when post-launch

## Design

Matches Portal V2: `#F47A48` primary, `#535353` charcoal, restrained cards, no fake green checks.
